Normalization rules:

* Normalize to ISO 8601 format when possible: YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY-MM, or YYYY.
* Interpret common historical entomological date formats, including:
  * Roman numeral months (e.g. `V-9-1996`)
  * Abbreviated months
  * Partial dates
  * Slash-separated numeric dates
* For slash-separated numeric dates using only Arabic numerals (e.g. `05/03/98`), interpret as MM/DD/YY
  unless other contextual evidence strongly suggests otherwise.
* For two-digit years: if the year value is greater than 26, interpret as 19XX; otherwise as 20XX.
* Examples:
  * `05/03/98` → `1998-05-03`
  * `07/14/05` → `2005-07-14`
  * `V-9-1996` → `1996-05-09`

Date selection rules:

* Museum labels may contain multiple dates associated with different events.
* Prioritize specimen collection dates over accession, determination, or provenance dates.
* Labels may contain abbreviations such as:
  * `ex` = from / out of
  * `em.` = emerged
* Dates associated with emergence events (`em.`) are often biologically relevant and may be used as `eventDate`.
* Do not interpret `ex` as indicating a collection event date by itself.
* If multiple dates are present, prefer the date most directly associated with:
  * collection, capture, rearing emergence, or field acquisition.
* Ignore accession and determination dates unless no other plausible collection-event date exists.
* If no collection date is present or confidently identifiable, return null.
* Do not hallucinate dates.
